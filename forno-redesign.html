Our Story

A single oven, and a stubborn idea.

Forno started with one wood-fired oven and a refusal to cut corners. Dough rested overnight. Sauces reduced for hours. Nothing left the pass until it was right.

A decade later the recipes haven't changed — only the number of people at the table. Our kitchen still shapes every pasta by hand, still fires every pizza on stone, and still stays open long after the city goes quiet.

Why Forno
